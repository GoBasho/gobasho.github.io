on one shared map.

Everyone on the trip adds their stays, plans, and must-sees. Basho lays them on a map, builds each person's day-by-day itinerary, suggests smart day plans from things that sit near each other, splits the expenses, and shows where your paths cross.

🗺️ One map, whole group

Stays, activities, transit, and photo spots, color-coded per person — with live sync, so edits appear as they happen.

💡 It plans with you

Drop in everything you want to do — flexible dates welcome — and it clusters what's near each other into ready-made days, checking the weather on the way.

💰 Costs that add up

Day-by-day cost estimates in local currency (converted to yours), plus group expense splitting that works out who owes whom.

✈️ Built for the real trip

Country-aware prep checklists, flight boards, calendar export, printable day sheets — and it works offline once you've loaded your trip.

Free and open source. Your trip's data lives in a private database and is visible only to people who have your trip's invite link — don't put passport numbers in it. Take your data anytime with the JSON export in the Trips menu. Found a bug or want a feature? Tell us here.
